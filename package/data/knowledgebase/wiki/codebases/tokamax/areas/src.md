# Area: `tokamax/_src`

## Purpose
`tokamax/_src` is the whole library: every op, its backends, and the shared machinery they
run on. Each op is a subclass of [`Op`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/op.py#L121 "tokamax/_src/ops/op#Op"), which owns argument binding,
config selection (explicit -> autotuning cache -> heuristic), the device check and the
custom-VJP wiring; the per-op `api.py` keeps an ordered registry of implementations and tries
them in turn. That framework is [op-framework](../concepts/op-framework.md), and
[kernel-catalog](../concepts/kernel-catalog.md) lists every op x backend with its purpose,
hardware gate and dispatch order - read it first to find which kernel serves an op on TPU.

The load-bearing TPU mechanisms are the per-op Pallas Mosaic TPU kernels:
[dot-product-attention](../concepts/dot-product-attention.md) and
[splash-attention](../concepts/splash-attention.md) for attention,
[ragged-dot-gmm](../concepts/ragged-dot-gmm.md) and [gmm-v2](../concepts/gmm-v2.md) for MoE
grouped matmul, [fused-moe](../concepts/fused-moe.md) and
[ragged-gather-reduce](../concepts/ragged-gather-reduce.md) for MoE dispatch/combine,
[batched-ragged-paged-attention](../concepts/batched-ragged-paged-attention.md) and
[multi-head-latent-attention](../concepts/multi-head-latent-attention.md) for serving,
[kimi-delta-attention](../concepts/kimi-delta-attention.md) and
[gated-delta-rule](../concepts/gated-delta-rule.md) for linear attention,
[linear-softmax-cross-entropy](../concepts/linear-softmax-cross-entropy.md) for the loss, and
[tpu-topk](../concepts/tpu-topk.md). Around them sit [autotuning](../concepts/autotuning.md),
[benchmarking-and-serialization](../concepts/benchmarking-and-serialization.md) and the shared
TPU plumbing in [mosaic-tpu-helpers](../concepts/mosaic-tpu-helpers.md).

What the area hands out is the public `tokamax.*` surface (re-exported from these modules)
and, through [torch-tpu-bridge](../concepts/torch-tpu-bridge.md), two of its TPU kernels to
PyTorch.

## How the units connect
Every op unit depends on the framework unit (`ops/op.py`) and on `autotuning`; nothing in
the framework depends on a particular op. Op units do not call each other, with two
exceptions: dot-product attention's TPU backend reuses the splash kernels, and
`triangle_multiplication` composes `layer_norm` and `gated_linear_unit`. The units with no
page of their own:

- **`tokamax/_src` (root)** - shared utilities: typing ([`jaxtyped`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/jaxtyping.py#L35 "tokamax/_src/jaxtyping#jaxtyped")),
  precision, quantized arrays, batching/vmap helpers, HLO introspection, config flags, and
  GPU helpers. The TPU-relevant parts are explained on
  [mosaic-tpu-helpers](../concepts/mosaic-tpu-helpers.md) and
  [benchmarking-and-serialization](../concepts/benchmarking-and-serialization.md).
- **`ops/normalization`, `ops/gated_linear_unit`** - GPU-only kernels:
  [`layer_norm`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/normalization/api.py#L46 "tokamax/_src/ops/normalization/api#layer_norm") has a Pallas Triton backend and
  [`gated_linear_unit`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/gated_linear_unit/api.py#L64 "tokamax/_src/ops/gated_linear_unit/api#gated_linear_unit") has Triton, Mosaic GPU and
  CuTe DSL backends. On TPU both run their XLA reference implementation; see
  [kernel-catalog](../concepts/kernel-catalog.md).
- **`ops/ragged_gather`, `ops/ragged_scatter`** - MoE token permutation on TPU v5+:
  [`RaggedGather`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_gather/base.py#L34 "tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_gather/base#RaggedGather") registers two Pallas TPU kernels
  (`mosaic_tpu_v2` = [`PallasV2TpuRaggedGather`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_gather/pallas_mosaic_v2_tpu.py#L27 "tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_gather/pallas_mosaic_v2_tpu#PallasV2TpuRaggedGather")
  is tried first), and [`RaggedScatter`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_scatter/base.py#L40 "tokamax/_src/ops/ragged_scatter/base#RaggedScatter") has a TPU
  kernel gated on SparseCore. Siblings of
  [ragged-gather-reduce](../concepts/ragged-gather-reduce.md).
- **`ops/flex_attention`** - [`FlexAttention`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/flex_attention/base.py#L45 "tokamax/_src/ops/flex_attention/base#FlexAttention")
  (score/mask modifier callables) is XLA-only on every platform.
- **`ops/triangle_multiplication`** -
  [`triangle_multiplication`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/triangle_multiplication/api.py#L33 "tokamax/_src/ops/triangle_multiplication/api#triangle_multiplication") (AlphaFold-style
  triangle update) is XLA-only; it forwards a `triton` choice to its inner norm/GLU calls.
- **`ops/experimental/fused_moe_rs`** - internal:
  [`gmm_v2_fused_rs`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/ops/experimental/fused_moe_rs/gmm_fused_rs_nodedup.py#L1327 "tokamax/_src/ops/experimental/fused_moe_rs/gmm_fused_rs_nodedup#gmm_v2_fused_rs") fuses
  gather, both expert GMMs (on the [gmm-v2](../concepts/gmm-v2.md) kernel), the activation and
  an ICI reduce-scatter in one TPU kernel; not registered as an op.
- **`ops/experimental/tpu`** - the parent of the splash and top-k directories, each with its
  own page.
- **`pallas`** - [`pallas_call`](https://github.com/openxla/tokamax/blob/3a0a3a34a5ccb8a6ac71f343d9cc44649d73db68/tokamax/_src/pallas/block.py#L271 "tokamax/_src/pallas/block#pallas_call") and grid grouping: GPU-side
  (Triton / Mosaic GPU) BlockSpec helpers; no Mosaic TPU kernel uses them.
