SparseCore is a per-chip accelerator block for sparse/embedding workloads. Counts per
chip: 4 on [v5p](../hardware/tpu-v5p.md)
([docs](../sources/2026-08-cloud-tpu-v5p-docs.md)), 2 on
[v6e](../hardware/tpu-v6e.md) ([docs](../sources/2026-08-cloud-tpu-v6e-docs.md)),
4 on [TPU7x](../hardware/tpu-7x.md) — 2 per chiplet
([docs](../sources/2026-08-cloud-tpu-7x-docs.md)).

*Stub — mechanism, programming model, and workload guidance await a dedicated source.*
